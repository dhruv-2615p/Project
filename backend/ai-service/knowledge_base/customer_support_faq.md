# Customer Support Policies and FAQs

## Account Management

### How do I create an account?

To create an account:

1. Visit our website at www.example.com
2. Click "Sign Up" in the top right corner
3. Enter your email address and create a password
4. Verify your email by clicking the link we send you
5. Complete your profile with your name and preferences
   Account creation is free and takes less than 2 minutes.

### How do I reset my password?

To reset your password:

1. Go to the login page
2. Click "Forgot Password?"
3. Enter your registered email address
4. Check your email for a reset link (check spam folder if not found)
5. Click the link and create a new password
6. Your new password must be at least 8 characters with one number and one special character

### How do I update my profile information?

To update your profile:

1. Log in to your account
2. Click on your profile icon in the top right
3. Select "Account Settings"
4. Update your information (name, email, phone, address)
5. Click "Save Changes"
   Changes take effect immediately.

### How do I delete my account?

To delete your account:

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to Account Settings > Privacy
3. Click "Delete Account" at the bottom
4. Confirm your decision
5. Enter your password to verify
   Note: Account deletion is permanent and all your data will be removed within 30 days.

## Billing and Payments

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept the following payment methods:

- Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover)
- Debit Cards
- PayPal
- Bank Transfer (for annual subscriptions)
- Apple Pay and Google Pay (on mobile)

### How do I view my billing history?

To view your billing history:

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to Account Settings > Billing
3. Click "Billing History"
4. You can download invoices as PDF

### How do I update my payment method?

To update your payment method:

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to Account Settings > Billing
3. Click "Payment Methods"
4. Click "Add New" or edit an existing method
5. Enter your new payment details
6. Click "Save"

### What is your refund policy?

Our refund policy:

- Full refund within 30 days of purchase for any reason
- Pro-rated refund for annual subscriptions cancelled after 30 days
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days
- Refunds go back to the original payment method
  To request a refund, contact support@example.com

### Why was my payment declined?

Common reasons for declined payments:

- Insufficient funds
- Expired card
- Incorrect card details
- Bank security block
  To resolve: Try a different payment method or contact your bank.

## Technical Support

### How do I report a bug?

To report a bug:

1. Click the "Help" button in the app
2. Select "Report a Bug"
3. Describe the issue in detail
4. Include screenshots if possible
5. Submit the report
   Our team will investigate within 24-48 hours.
   You can also email bugs@example.com

### The app is not loading. What should I do?

If the app is not loading:

1. Check your internet connection
2. Clear your browser cache and cookies
3. Try a different browser
4. Disable any browser extensions
5. Restart your device
   If the issue persists, contact our support team.

### How do I clear my cache?

To clear cache:

- Chrome: Settings > Privacy > Clear Browsing Data
- Firefox: Options > Privacy > Clear Data
- Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
- Edge: Settings > Privacy > Clear Browsing Data

### What browsers do you support?

We support the following browsers:

- Google Chrome (latest 2 versions)
- Mozilla Firefox (latest 2 versions)
- Safari (latest 2 versions)
- Microsoft Edge (latest 2 versions)
  We recommend using Chrome for the best experience.

### How do I enable notifications?

To enable notifications:

1. Go to Account Settings > Notifications
2. Toggle on the types of notifications you want
3. Allow browser notifications when prompted
4. Check that your system notifications are enabled

5. Save your backup codes

## Product Features

### How do I create a new project?

To create a project:

1. Click the "+" button on the dashboard
2. Enter a project name
3. Choose a template or start blank
4. Invite team members (optional)
5. Click "Create Project"

### How do I invite team members?

To invite team members:

1. Open your project
2. Click "Settings" > "Team"
3. Click "Invite Member"
4. Enter their email address
5. Select their role (Admin, Editor, Viewer)
6. Click "Send Invitation"

### How do I share my work?

To share your work:

1. Open the item you want to share
2. Click the "Share" button
3. Choose sharing option:
   - Private link (view only)
   - Public link (anyone can view)
   - Direct share (to specific people)
4. Copy and share the link

### How do I export my data?

To export your data:

1. Open your project
2. Click "Export" in the menu
3. Choose format (PDF, CSV, Excel, JSON)
4. Select what to include
5. Click "Download"
